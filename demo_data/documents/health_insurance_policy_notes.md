## Medical Billing Dispute Coverage

### Covered Scenarios

The policy covers medical billing overcharges and billing disputes under the following conditions:

1. **Billing Discrepancy Threshold:** Disputes involving overcharges **greater than Rs 2,000** are eligible for claim processing

2. **Dispute Timeline:** Claims must be filed within **30 days** of the billing date

3. **Required Documentation:**
   - Original hospital bill
   - Bank payment confirmation
   - Written explanation of the discrepancy

### Claim Process

1. Document the exact amount billed vs. amount that should have been charged
2. Contact hospital billing department first for resolution attempt
3. If unresolved, file insurance claim with all supporting documents
4. Insurance company will review and either:
   - Reimburse the overcharged amount directly to the policy holder
   - Negotiate with the hospital on behalf of the policy holder

### Coverage Limits

- Maximum reimbursement for billing disputes: Rs 50,000 per incident
- Annual limit for all dispute claims: Rs 200,000

## Important Notes

Always retain copies of all medical bills and payment receipts for at least 24 months. Billing disputes can arise from coding errors, duplicate charges, or services not rendered.
